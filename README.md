Entry for the make.fun bounty [The Best Meal You Ever Had](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pangg7-the-best-meal-you-ever-had-a3ac0f85).

## The entry

One sentence, at most 200 characters, about the best meal ever had. No
title, no explanation, no list of courses — just one sentence that has
to stand alone. Ten fresh candidates are scored against the judge's
criteria in order — a specific meal, sensory detail, an emotional turn
in the last few words, freshness, reads well aloud — and the winner is
written to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The chosen sentence is written to `/output/entry.txt` (single sentence,
at most 200 characters).
